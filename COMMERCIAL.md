# Commercial Licensing

Gatewarden is available as open-source software under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

If that license works for your use case, you can use the OSS repository directly.

## When you may want a commercial license

Contact us if you need any of the following:

- closed-source modifications
- hosted or closed-source SaaS use
- OEM or white-label distribution
- private enterprise integrations
- commercial support or SLA
- custom delivery or deployment help

## OSS and enterprise model

Gatewarden is maintained as:

- one OSS mainline repository
- one private enterprise extension layer

The enterprise edition is intended to extend the OSS core, not replace it with a long-lived private fork.

## What to include when reaching out

Please include:

- company name
- deployment model
- whether you need closed-source use
- whether you need hosted/SaaS rights
- whether you need OEM or white-label rights
- expected scale
- support requirements
